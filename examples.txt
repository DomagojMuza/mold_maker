make_mold.py -- command examples
=================================

BLENDER = "C:\Program Files\Blender Foundation\Blender 5.1\blender.exe"

----- BASIC -----

4-piece mold (default):
  & $BLENDER --background --python make_mold.py -- input.stl

2-piece mold:
  & $BLENDER --background --python make_mold.py -- input.stl --two

Output to specific folder:
  & $BLENDER --background --python make_mold.py -- input.stl C:\output\mold1

----- WALL THICKNESS -----

Thin temp mold  (wall = SHELL_OFFSET - CORE_OFFSET = 4.5 - 3.0 = 1.5mm):
  & $BLENDER --background --python make_mold.py -- input.stl --shell 4.5 --core 3.0

Standard mold  (wall = 7.0 - 3.0 = 4mm):
  & $BLENDER --background --python make_mold.py -- input.stl --shell 7.0 --core 3.0

Tight cavity  (wall = 5.0 - 2.0 = 3mm, thinner silicone gap):
  & $BLENDER --background --python make_mold.py -- input.stl --shell 5.0 --core 2.0

----- POUR / VENTS -----

Bottom pour (no reservoir, no vents -- pour through open base):
  & $BLENDER --background --python make_mold.py -- input.stl --pour bottom

Custom funnel mouth radius:
  & $BLENDER --background --python make_mold.py -- input.stl --funnel 8.0

No vents:
  & $BLENDER --background --python make_mold.py -- input.stl --novents

More vent holes (up to 5):
  & $BLENDER --background --python make_mold.py -- input.stl --vent-n 5

Smaller vent bore (0.8mm):
  & $BLENDER --background --python make_mold.py -- input.stl --vent 0.8

----- STAB FEET -----

Force 4 feet only:
  & $BLENDER --background --python make_mold.py -- input.stl --stab-n 4

Cylinder feet instead of cones:
  & $BLENDER --background --python make_mold.py -- input.stl --stab-shape cyl

No stab feet:
  & $BLENDER --background --python make_mold.py -- input.stl --nostab

----- REGISTRATION PINS -----

No flange, no pins (bare split):
  & $BLENDER --background --python make_mold.py -- input.stl --noflange

Custom pin radius:
  & $BLENDER --background --python make_mold.py -- input.stl --pin 3.0

Fixed pin count per interface:
  & $BLENDER --background --python make_mold.py -- input.stl --pins-n 3

----- COMBINED EXAMPLES -----

Thin 2-piece temp mold, no cradle, bottom pour:
  & $BLENDER --background --python make_mold.py -- input.stl --two --shell 4.5 --core 3.0 --pour bottom --nocradle

Full production 4-piece, thick wall, more vents:
  & $BLENDER --background --python make_mold.py -- input.stl --shell 8.0 --core 3.0 --vent-n 5 --pins-n 3

High-detail fine voxel (slower, sharper geometry):
  & $BLENDER --background --python make_mold.py -- input.stl --voxel 0.3 --target 12000

cap_high_res test (quick defaults):
  & $BLENDER --background --python make_mold.py -- cap_high_res.stl test_out
